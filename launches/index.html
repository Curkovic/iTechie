>_ mission brief // AIShenzhou 24 lifts off on a Long March 2F/G from Jiuquan, inserting a crew into Low Earth Orbit. It carries Pakistan’s first astronaut on a short flight and a Chinese crew member slated for a year‑long stay, making the mission a geopolitical milestone.

Shenzhou 24 will be the 24th flight of the Shenzhou program. The first astronaut from Pakistan, Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, will fly on this on a short duration mission, with Chinese astronaut Zhu Yangzhu of Shenzhou 23 to undergo a year long expedition and stay on the CSS in exchange.
