FIVE NOTES ON A WHEEL BESIDE THE SUNMiles · 21 Nov 2026

From this terminal on the Adriatic, the view is changing. On Zadar's Riva, a few steps from the Greeting to the Sun, crews have started assembling a panoramic wheel for Advent. It is the first time the city has had one. Some observations, filed in order. 1. The specifications are precise. Just under 29 metres tall, 21 closed and heated gondolas, room for 84 people, and a ride of at least seven minutes. Rarely has a festive object arrived with such a clear datasheet. 2. Split wanted one too. Its public call asked for a wheel at least 21 metres tall on the Riva by St Francis church, with an estimated value of nearly 219,000 euros. One company bid. The bid was rejected because the wheel needed 40 kilowatts and the call allowed 20, and because some documents lacked certified Croatian translations. A season of lights, defeated by a power rating and a missing stamp. 3. Zadar pays nothing. The tourist board was prepared to cover a 20,000 euro installation fee, but the operator waived it, because it will earn its money from tickets. The board's conditions were that small children and children with disabilities ride free. A modest contract, quietly well made.
