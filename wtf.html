What is this?

iTechie.eu is a small website that keeps an eye on Zadar and the world around it, and tells you, every hour, how worried you should be. Usually not very. Sometimes a rock passes close.

I built it for myself, so I'd have everything useful in one place in the morning while I drink my makijato: is the water on, is the power on, is the bura coming, what's flying overhead, what's launching into space. By accident, it turned out other people might find it useful too. Plus a few things that are just for fun.

Who's behind it?

Me, Mate Ćurković. I'm a System Administrator. By day I look after Linux servers, and by night apparently I build more of them. I'm a big fan of open source, and this whole site runs on it: free tools, public data, and a Linux server at home doing the heavy lifting.

Why does it look like this?

As you can see, I like the cyberpunk vibe. I'm all in on the end of the world and the AI overlords taking over. This site is half satire, half what I actually believe. Which half is which, I'll let you decide.

How does it work?

Nobody updates this page by hand. Every hour the site rebuilds itself. It checks the weather, the air, the water and power notices, the sky and the news, and puts it all together. If something breaks, it keeps the last good version and tries again next hour. Like a good server should.

Who writes the words?

Some of it is me. A lot of it is AI. One AI writes a column about the world, another one files a daily damage report on humanity, and a few others add comments to the data. They work on their own and they're not always right, which is half the fun. The sources page lists where everything comes from.

What it's not

It's not official, it's not news and it's not advice. If the threat level ever says 5, please also check what the actual authorities say.

Why?

Because I can. Because a sysadmin needs a playground. And because if the world does end, at least it'll be well monitored.
